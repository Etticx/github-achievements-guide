# Public Sponsor

You earn it by sponsoring a person or organization through **GitHub Sponsors**. It has only one tier.

Time needed: 3 minutes. You need a payment method, and it costs whatever amount you choose to give.

## GUI

1. Go to the profile of someone whose work you use, and click the **Sponsor** button (heart icon). You can also browse [github.com/sponsors/explore](https://github.com/sponsors/explore).
2. Choose **One-time** or **Monthly**, and pick an amount. A one-time sponsorship is enough for the badge.
3. Keep the sponsorship **public**. A private sponsorship may not count, since the badge is "Public" Sponsor.
4. Finish checkout.

## CLI

None. Payments are made on the website only.

## Who to sponsor

Pick a library or tool you actually use. A small one-time amount is enough.
